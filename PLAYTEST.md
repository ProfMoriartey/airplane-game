# Playtesting Red Ace

Numbers from the balance tools (`tools/balance/`) only go so far: they can't tell us what
is confusing, frustrating or fun. That needs people playing.

## What the game records

Everything is saved on the tester's computer, in the **playtest folder**
(Settings > Gameplay > *Open playtest folder*):

- `results.csv` - one line per mission, duel or Quick Battle: result, time, score, kills,
  damage taken, shots and hits, difficulty, aim assist and aircraft. Opens in any spreadsheet.
- `feedback.txt` - whatever the tester writes in the **Feedback** form (on the debrief
  screen and in the pause menu): how hard it felt, how much fun it was, and comments.

If the game crashes, the log files (Settings > Gameplay > *Open log folder*) say why.

## Running a session

1. Give the tester the game with a fresh pilot (Pilots screen > Create pilot).
2. **Don't help.** Watch quietly and write down where they hesitate, get lost or die.
   If they ask how to do something, that's a finding - note it, then help.
3. Ask them to fill in the feedback form after each mission (it takes 20 seconds).
4. Afterwards ask:
   - What was the most fun moment? The most frustrating?
   - Was anything unclear - controls, objectives, where to go?
   - Did any mission feel too long, too empty or too hard?
   - Would you keep playing? What would make you stop?
5. Collect the playtest folder (zip it) and your notes.

## What to look for in the results

- **Retries**: the same mission failed many times in a row is a difficulty spike.
- **Time**: missions much longer than the others may feel empty or slow.
- **Accuracy** below about 10% means players struggle to hit - consider stronger aim assist
  by default or slower enemies early on.
- **Damage taken** near 100% on wins means the mission is barely survivable.
- **"Far too hard" / "Far too easy"** answers on the same mission from several testers.

## Testers to find

- Complete beginners (have never played a flight game) - for the first missions and controls.
- People who play flight or action games - for the later missions and aces.
- At least one person with a controller and one with only keyboard and mouse.
